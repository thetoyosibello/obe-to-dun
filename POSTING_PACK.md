## Cadence

One pin a day for the first two weeks, rotating the four creatives across the four boards,
then one every other day. Do not post all four on day one.

Titles carry the keyword, never the brand. "Obé Tó Dùn" means nothing to Pinterest search
and the research is unambiguous: product pins in this niche cap around 400 saves while
recipe pins run to 60,000.

## Next creative tier

Video wins this niche outright. Every pin above 5,000 saves is a 45 to 75 second vertical
cooking video, and the top linked pin in the whole category is a 72 second one. When mum
cooks, film the pot: hands only, locked off from above, open on the finished stew.
One cooking session is a month of pins.
